# Department conflicts

Log each conflict (writers' room log, or bible if about the look). Resolve by: "about"
sentence → power ledger → cost. Director rules in one line.

## Recurring in every series

| Conflict | Pull | Usual resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Designer vs time | Detail vs ~2,000-character scenes | Detail lives in lock sets, built once; in dark looks, only where light falls |
| Camera vs living frame | Still camera vs "frozen reads as a bug" | Still camera, living world: atmosphere, breathing, flicker, moving holds |
| Screenwriter vs voice | Behavior vs voice | Series-level choice (questions.md #1), never mixed between episodes |
| Sound vs picture order | Audio-led timing vs picture lock | Scratch audio leads the animatic; final audio follows lock |
| Slow burn vs Motion lengths | Patient scenes vs 300 s cap | Episode = chain of Motion films; slowness in edit and holds |
| Fidelity vs originality | Study great films vs resemble none | Triangulate; signatures under "don't copy" |
| Consistency vs new idea mid-series | Lock vs a better look in episode five | Lock wins in the episode; the idea becomes an approved bump |

## Motion defaults a series may override (record in the bible)

| Motion default | Override | When |
|---|---|---|
| Flat color, no gradients | Gradients for light and darkness only, from palette variables | Motivated or expressionist light |
| 60 fps | 24 fps (poses read as film); 30 if slow pans judder | Film-like, puppet, cut-out looks |
| Never below about −30 dB | Silence once or twice, for the biggest beat | Drama, comedy |
| Upbeat grooves | Beds, no groove | Drama, suspense |
| No only-black/red/white-cream frames | Keep it; noir uses amber, teal, ochre, smoke-blue, bone, red as small accent | Unless the user asks (`allow_black_red_white`) |
| Every close-up has motion | Motion inside the frame, not the camera | Rationed-camera series |

Motion's hard rules never yield: `motion-integration.md`.
