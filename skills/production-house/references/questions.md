# Questions

After research, before the writers' room. ≤5, one tap each, each with a dossier default,
recommended option first with a few words of why (question tool when available). Ask only
what changes the film and research can't settle.

**Not asked here:** the look and palette (answered with pictures at Gate 2), the ledger and
"about" (proposed in the screenplay at Gate 1).

## Series-level (once; stored in the bible)

1. **Voice model.** *Silent + intertitles and score* (story by behavior, objects, light);
   *narrated in post* (scenes leave air for lines); *full dialogue in post* (staged for
   talk; mostly outside Motion).
2. **Audience.** Children, general, adult. Bounds tone, pacing, what's implied. Default
   from genre.
3. **Fidelity.** *Stylized* or *strict* (researched props, signage, costume; costs more
   per set and character).

## Film-level (skip what script or lock settles)

4. **Format.** Single scene (20–60 s), sequence (1–3 min), episode segment (≤5 min).
   Sets Motion's Length; longer = a chain of films.
5. **Gates.** Default all three. Ask only if the user signals hurry; then offer to merge
   Gates 1 and 2.

## Never ask

"What mood/style?" · "Should it be cinematic?" · anything the script, lock or an earlier
answer says · two questions on one decision · anything during the build.
